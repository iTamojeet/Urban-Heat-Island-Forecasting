How to read this
  • Heat and vegetation are mean March-May values at 120 m (one cell is 1.44 ha), from Landsat 8/9 over the usable months of 2014-2025.
  • Cooling = local NDVI response × NDVI gain. The local response comes from how a cell's own LST moved with its own NDVI, with the city-wide mean held fixed. It is the cross-sectional slope (hot, bare areas vs green ones) that over-promises, because it mixes vegetation with urban form.
  • This is model sensitivity, not a causal estimate. One response is applied to every cell. Dense, low-vegetation cells probably respond less than average. July-September is not covered at all.
